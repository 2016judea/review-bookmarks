A table of books a writer actually borrowed from Sylvia Beach in Paris. A card for each book, a sign for the table, bookmarks for every title. Every fact is from her lending cards.

    Every fact on these cards is from Sylvia Beach's lending library cards, Shakespeare and Company, Paris, 1919–1941, published by the Shakespeare and Company Project, Princeton University Library, CC BY 4.0. Each card names its lending card. On a writer's table we chose which books; on a subject table a model chose them from the list. Neither wrote a word of the facts.

    "In print" means a new copy was listed at Powell's, or a print edition is listed by Penguin Random House, on the date shown. "Not confirmed" means we didn't find one, not that it's out of print; check your distributor. Covers from Open Library.
